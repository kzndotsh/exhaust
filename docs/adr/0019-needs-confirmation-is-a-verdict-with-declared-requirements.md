# `needs-confirmation` is a Verdict, carrying what would resolve it

A sixth Verdict exists alongside confirmed, false-positive, duplicate, fixed, and accepted-risk: `needs-confirmation`. A Finding in this state declares, as data, what evidence would be sufficient to move it to `confirmed` (for example, the kind of Oracle check required, or the environment it must be reproduced in) and may cite supporting evidence gathered so far, distinct from evidence that would contradict it. A later promotion to `confirmed` is only valid once evidence meeting those declared requirements exists, with nothing contradicting it on record.

Our existing five Verdicts assume every investigation resolves one way or another, but in practice an agent frequently finds something real and interesting before it's in a position to say which of the other five applies. Without a state for that, a Finding either gets force-fit into one of the five prematurely or sits with no Verdict at all, which our append-only model doesn't otherwise allow. A reverse-engineering toolkit audited for this research has exactly this concept, as a durable record distinct from its evidence, carrying its own open/investigating/blocked/resolved lifecycle and declared resolution requirements rather than a bare "pending" flag with no criteria attached. Pairing the Verdict with its own resolution criteria is what makes this more than a parking lot: closing it later is a check against declared requirements, not a judgment call made fresh each time.
